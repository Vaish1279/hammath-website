THE STORY BEHIND HAMMATH

An original idea.
Built with creativity.

HAMMATH began as an independent creative concept: making everyday maths practice feel more playful, focused and approachable. The game concept, its direction and the experience are the creator's own work, developed beyond a classroom exercise as a personal project.

This is an evolving project. Features and details may improve as the game grows.

GOOD TO KNOW

Quick answers.

Is HAMMATH free to play?

The currently published game can be opened from the Play button. If pricing or paid features are introduced later, they will be clearly identified before use.

How do I install it?

Open the game in a supported mobile browser, open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home Screen if the option is available.

Where is my progress saved?

Current game progress is stored locally on the device/browser. Clearing browser data or changing devices may remove or not transfer that progress.

Can I share feedback?

Yes. Use the support email shown in the Contact section once the creator's chosen contact address has been added.
