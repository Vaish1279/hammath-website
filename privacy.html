Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This is a starter privacy notice for the current HAMMATH website and game. Review and update it before enabling analytics, advertising, forms, accounts or other data collection.

Information collected

The current game is designed to save gameplay progress locally in the browser/device. This website does not currently provide user accounts, a contact form, analytics or advertising. If you choose to email the creator, your email address and message will be processed by your email provider and received by the contact recipient.

Local storage

The website may use browser local storage to remember when it was first visited and whether a feedback reminder was dismissed. This is used only to show an optional on-site reminder after 30 days on a later visit. It does not send a push notification or transmit this information to a server.

Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and opens the game hosted on Vercel. Those providers may process technical data under their own privacy terms. If analytics, ads, embedded services or additional providers are added, this policy must be updated before launch.

Children and contact

Do not submit sensitive personal information. For privacy questions or requests, contact the creator using the verified contact email published on the website.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand how visitors interact with the HAMMATH website and to improve its content and functionality. Google Analytics may collect information such as pages visited, interactions, browser type, device information, approximate location, referral information and other usage data. It may use cookies and similar technologies to measure website activity. This information helps us understand website traffic and improve the overall user experience. We do not intentionally send personally identifiable information, such as names, email addresses or passwords, to Google Analytics. Google processes information collected through its services in accordance with its privacy policy. For more information, please visit Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. You can also learn about opting out of Google Analytics measurement here: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout/
